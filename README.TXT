This modpack is open for modification so as long as you don't distribute it open to the public without my permission. Sharing in between a group of friends is fine.


# Modification Of the Mod List
If you happen to modify the modpack and remove or add mods, you may not receive support if it has many or known buggy mods. You may be asked to revert back to the original modpack state. I will try my best to help you tho. I just ask that you tell me what mods you added or removed, and what configs you had to change.

# Changing of NEI
Also, you may have noticed the original NEI is used instead of the GTNH Fork. While GTNH NEI seems to be the norm, I will not use it. This is due to the fact the fork has removed old features from old NEI, and overall has been buggier than the original in many ways. You should not use GTNH NEI for my pack unless you need bookmarks. Any NEI issues using the fork is not expected to be handled by me.
